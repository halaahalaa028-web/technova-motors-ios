**Description** (≤4000)
TechNova Motors is the operating system for car showrooms and dealerships. Your whole team works from one app — on the showroom floor, in the office or on the road.

STOCK
• Every car in one list: price, status, photos, documents and full history
• Purchase approvals, car covers and payment orders generated for you
• Reserved, sold and delivered — always know where each car stands

CRM & SALES
• Leads from WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and walk-ins in one pipeline
• Follow-ups that don't slip: due today, overdue and upcoming per salesperson
• Reservations, sales orders, receipts and handover forms in a tap
• CRM reports: pipeline, sources, conversion and team performance, exported to PDF or Excel

ONE INBOX FOR EVERY CHAT
• WhatsApp Business, Instagram and Messenger together
• Assign chats, quick replies, labels and voice notes
• See which salesperson answers and how fast

MONEY
• Treasury, cash flow and safe in real time
• Every expense on the car it belongs to, so margins are true
• Partners, commissions and account statements

TEAM
• Attendance with location check-in at the showroom and face check-in
• Roles and permissions for every employee
• Notifications for new leads, messages and approvals
• Lock the app with Face ID

TechNova Motors works in Arabic and English, in light and dark mode.

Sign in with the TechNova account your showroom gave you.

## App Privacy (App Store Connect ▸ App Privacy)

Tracking: **No** — no data is used to track people across other companies' apps or sites, no ad SDKs.

Data collected, all **linked to the user**, all for **App Functionality** only:

| Apple category | What it is in TechNova Motors |
|---|---|
| Contact Info ▸ Name, Email Address, Phone Number | the employee's account; customers' names and numbers in the CRM |
| User Content ▸ Photos or Videos, Audio Data, Customer Support, Other User Content | car photos and documents, voice notes, chat messages |
| Location ▸ Precise Location | attendance check-in at the showroom (only while checking in) |
| Identifiers ▸ User ID | the account id |
| Other Data ▸ Other Data Types | the face template used for face check-in (attendance) |
| Financial Info ▸ Other Financial Info | showroom treasury, deals and payments entered by staff |

Not collected: Health, Browsing History, Search History, Purchases, Diagnostics, Advertising Data.

## Notes for the App Review team

Paste into App Store Connect ▸ App Review Information ▸ Notes. Fill the sign-in
fields with the review account (ask the owner for its password — never put it in a
file).

> TechNova Motors is a business tool for car showrooms in Egypt: staff manage the
> showroom's car stock, customer leads, WhatsApp/Instagram/Messenger chats, deals,
> treasury and attendance.
>
> Sign-in: accounts are created by each showroom for its employees. Use the review
> account in the sign-in fields; it opens the demo showroom "Motor Hub Demo" with
> sample data.
>
> Native features: push notifications for new leads, messages and approvals
> (Apple Push Notification service); Face ID lock when the app opens or returns
> after 3 minutes (toggle in the menu ▸ "Face ID / fingerprint lock"); haptic tab
> bar; camera for face check-in and car photos; location for attendance check-in;
> microphone for voice notes; an offline screen when there is no connection.
>
> No purchases: the app sells nothing. Showroom subscriptions are arranged by the
> business outside the app; inside the app there are no prices, plans or payment
> links.
>
> Account deletion: menu ▸ "Delete my account" (also at
> https://motors.technovaos.com/delete-account). An owner account holds a whole
> showroom's records, so deletion is completed by our team within 30 days; the
> screen says so.

Export compliance: the app uses only standard HTTPS encryption —
`ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` is already in Info.plist, so no question is asked.
